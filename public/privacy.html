Privacy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This page explains what MovieNest stores and who else is involved. We keep it to what the site needs to work.

What we store

  • Username and device ID. You pick a username, and your browser creates a random device ID so your username stays yours. There are no accounts, emails or passwords.
  • Rooms. Room code, name, the selected video, playback position, and the host’s random ID and room settings.
  • Chat messages. The text, your username and the time sent. Anyone in the room can read them.
  • Search results. Saved search results are reused for a few days to save quota. They contain no personal information.
  • On your device only. Your Watch Later list, watch history, and your device ID are kept in your browser’s local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data, show ads, or build advertising profiles.

Services that help run MovieNest

  • Supabase stores rooms, messages and usernames and delivers live updates. Its servers are in London.
  • Vercel hosts the website and, like most hosts, may log technical details such as IP addresses.
  • YouTube provides the player and search. Its embedded player can set cookies and collect data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage Google permissions at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The Internet Archive delivers its video files directly to your browser.

How long we keep things

Rooms and messages stay until we clean them up or delete them on request. We may remove old rooms from time to time.

Your choices

To ask for a username, room or messages to be deleted, email victorwilz112@gmail.com with your username or room code. To clear what’s stored on your device, clear your browser’s site data for MovieNest.

Children

MovieNest isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
