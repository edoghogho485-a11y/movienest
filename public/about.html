About MovieNest

Last updated: November 14, 2026

MovieNest is a free place to watch videos together with friends. Make a private room, share the invite link, and everyone’s player stays in sync, so a play, pause or jump to another scene happens for the whole room at once. There’s live chat and reactions in every room, and you can also watch alone.

Where the videos come from

MovieNest doesn’t upload, store or host any videos. Everything you watch is streamed from the original source:

  • YouTube, using videos that their owners allow to be embedded.
  • The Internet Archive, which holds public-domain and freely shared films.

If a video disappears from its source, it stops working in MovieNest too.

How rooms work

A room has an invite code and link. Anyone who has them can join, so share them only with people you trust. Hosts can lock a room or limit who controls playback.
